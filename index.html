Questions

Good to know.

Who can use Vinto?

Licensed real estate agents and brokers at brokerages and teams that sign up. Accounts are invite-only: the broker approves each agent.

Does it replace our compliance or e-signature tool?

No. Vinto is the agent’s daily app and works alongside the tools your brokerage already uses. It prepares Georgia forms with signature boxes for DocuSign and gets a complete file ready for broker review.

Where do property details come from?

From the documents the agent adds and from public county tax records. Vinto is not connected to an MLS.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. The AI that reads contracts runs under commercial terms that forbid training on your data, and sensitive documents like wire instructions and bank statements are kept away from it.

What phones does it run on?

iPhone. The client portal works in any browser, on any phone or computer.

Can we cancel?

Anytime. Plans are month to month. Your data is yours: ask and we’ll send you a copy or delete it.
