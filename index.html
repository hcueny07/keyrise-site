What it does

One app for the whole transaction.

Built around how agents actually work, from the first call with a lead to the review request after closing.

i.

Contracts, read for you

Upload a purchase agreement and Keyrise fills in the deal: price, earnest money, contingencies, and the dates that come with them.

ii.

Deadlines that count themselves

Every Georgia contract date tracked, with reminders on your phone and a checklist for each side of the deal.

iii.

Clients, start to finish

New leads get a reply and follow-ups. Buyer consultations, showing tours and past-client check-ins, all in one place.

iv.

Listings without the busywork

A listing checklist from signed agreement to closing, plus listing details and comps read straight from your MLS reports.

v.

Signatures, ready to send

Signature, initial and date boxes added to Georgia forms in one tap, ready for your e-signature service.

vi.

A clean file for the broker

Each file is checked against the office's required documents before it goes to review, so brokers see what's missing at a glance.

Private by design

Your clients' business stays your business.

Keyrise was built for licensed professionals handling other people's biggest purchase. Privacy isn't a setting. It's the default.

  • Locked with Face ID. The app hides its screen in the app switcher.
  • Commission stays private. Prices and commission never appear on a screen a client might see.
  • Each agent sees only their own deals. Brokers see their office, never another brokerage's.
  • MLS data stays with the agent. Listing reports are read by the app's own code and never sent to AI.

Questions

Good to know.

Who can use Keyrise?

Licensed real estate agents and brokers. Accounts are invite-only: once you sign up, we set up your account and invite you, and brokers approve their own agents.

Does it work with my MLS and e-signature service?

Yes. Keyrise reads listing reports and CMAs you export from Remine, FMLS, Georgia MLS, Flexmls or Matrix, and prepares Georgia forms with signature boxes for DocuSign.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. The AI that reads contracts runs under commercial terms that forbid training on your data, and MLS listing reports are never sent to AI at all.

What phones does it run on?

iPhone today. Brokers can review their office from the same app.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Your data is yours: ask and we'll send you a copy or delete it.
