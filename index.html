Questions

Good to know.

Who can use Vinto?

Licensed real estate agents and brokers. Accounts are invite-only: once you sign up, we set up your account and invite you, and brokers approve their own agents.

Does it work with my MLS and e-signature service?

Yes. Vinto reads listing reports and CMAs you export from Remine, FMLS, Georgia MLS, Flexmls or Matrix, and prepares Georgia forms with signature boxes for DocuSign.

Is my data used to train AI?

No. The AI that reads contracts runs under commercial terms that forbid training on your data, and MLS listing reports are never sent to AI at all.

What phones does it run on?

iPhone today. Brokers review their office from the same app.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. Monthly plans stop at the end of the month. Your data is yours: ask and we'll send you a copy or delete it.
