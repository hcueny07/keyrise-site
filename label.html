Label an edited photo

Staged a room with AI or brightened the sky? Add a clear label before it goes on a listing or a post. Nothing is uploaded: the labeled copy is made on this phone, and your original photo is left as it was.

Keep both versions

Save the original, unedited photo with the transaction too. Many MLSs and brokerages require the original to be available next to an edited one. Ask your broker what your office requires.

A label tells buyers a photo was changed. It does not make a misleading edit OK: don’t remove power lines, cracks, neighbors or anything a buyer would want to know about.
