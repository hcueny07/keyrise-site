Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Vinto is a private app for licensed real estate agents at brokerages that sign up, and for independent agents we approve. This policy explains what the app stores, why, and who can see it.

Who we are

Vinto is built and run by Cueny Ventures LLC, a Georgia company. Questions about this policy go to hudsoncueny@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: the agent's name, email address and brokerage.
  • Transaction information agents add: property addresses, contracts and other documents, deadlines, notes, and their clients' names and contact details.
  • Listing reports agents add: MLS listing sheets and market reports an agent shares into the app. The app reads these with its own code, never with AI, and uses them only as the agent's MLS allows.
  • Device permissions the agent turns on: calendar (for showing reminders), contacts (to pick a client; only the contact the agent picks is saved), camera and photos (to scan documents and business cards), Face ID (to unlock the app; the face data never leaves the phone), and notifications.
  • Usage and crash information: how much the AI is used, for billing limits, and crash reports with names, phone numbers, emails, addresses and dollar amounts removed.
  • Billing: when you pay on this website, payment is handled by our payment processor. We see the plan, amount and billing email, never your full card number.

How we use it

Only to run the app for the agent and their brokerage: organizing transactions, sending deadline reminders, and answering the agent's questions with AI. We do not sell data, show ads, or use it for marketing.

Who can see it

  • Each agent sees only their own transactions and clients.
  • Brokerage admins see their own brokerage's office information and usage, not other brokerages'.
  • Restricted MLS fields, such as showing instructions and private remarks, are shown only to the agent.

Service providers

  • Supabase stores the app's data in an encrypted cloud database.
  • Anthropic provides the AI (Claude) that reads contracts and answers questions. Under its commercial terms, Anthropic may not train its models on the app's data. MLS listing reports are never sent to the AI.
  • Sentry receives crash reports, with personal details removed.
  • MLS and showing services provide listing and showing information only when the brokerage has approved them.
  • DocuSign: Vinto can add signature boxes to a form. The agent then uploads it to DocuSign under their own account, and DocuSign's own privacy policy applies there.
  • Payments on this website are processed by Stripe under its own privacy policy.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. The app locks with Face ID or the phone's passcode, hides its screen in the app switcher, and keeps commission and prices off screens a client might see.

Keeping and deleting data

Data is kept while the agent or brokerage uses Vinto. An agent or brokerage can ask for a copy of their data or for it to be deleted at any time by emailing hudsoncueny@gmail.com. When a brokerage or agent stops using the app, its data is deleted within 30 days.

Children

Vinto is for licensed real estate professionals only and is not meant for anyone under 18.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
