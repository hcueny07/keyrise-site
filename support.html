Getting started

  • Getting an account: Keyrise is invite-only. Your broker adds your work email, or we set you up when you sign up on the pricing page. Then open the app and tap “Create your account” with that email.
  • “This email hasn’t been invited”: use the exact email your broker added, or ask them to add it.
  • Forgot your password: on the sign-in screen tap “Forgot your password?”, then open the email on your iPhone and tap the link to set a new one.

Common questions

  • Adding a contract: open the house, tap Documents, and add the PDF or a photo. Keyrise fills in the price, parties and dates.
  • Listing reports and CMAs: export them as a PDF from your MLS or Remine, then open the house and tap “Listing sheet”.
  • DocuSign boxes: open the house, tap Documents, then “Add boxes and share”. Upload the result to DocuSign and choose to transform PDF fields.
